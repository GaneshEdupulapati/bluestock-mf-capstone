# Infosys Limited – Financial Statement Analysis

## 1. Company Overview

Infosys Limited is an Indian publicly listed technology and consulting company. Its primary listings are on the BSE and National Stock Exchange of India.

This analysis uses Infosys' consolidated financial statements for the financial year ended March 31, 2026.

All financial amounts below are in ₹ crore unless otherwise stated.

---

## 2. Profit and Loss Analysis

| Particular | FY 2025 | FY 2026 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue from operations | 1,62,990 | 1,78,650 | +9.6% |
| Operating Profit | 34,424 | 36,254 | +5.3% |
| Profit Before Tax | 37,608 | 39,995 | +6.3% |
| Net Profit | 26,750 | 29,474 | +10.2% |
| Basic EPS (₹) | 64.50 | 71.58 | +11.0% |

### Analysis

Infosys reported revenue of ₹1,78,650 crore in FY2026 compared with ₹1,62,990 crore in FY2025. This represents a 9.6% year-on-year increase.

Operating profit increased from ₹34,424 crore to ₹36,254 crore, representing 5.3% growth.

Net profit increased from ₹26,750 crore to ₹29,474 crore, representing 10.2% growth.

Basic EPS increased from ₹64.50 to ₹71.58, representing 11.0% growth.

Overall, the increase in revenue, profit and EPS indicates improved earnings performance during FY2026.

## 3. Balance Sheet Analysis

| Particular | FY 2025 | FY 2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Total Assets | 1,48,903 | 1,55,967 |
| Total Equity | 96,203 | 93,297 |
| Cash and Cash Equivalents | 24,455 | 22,201 |
| Non-current Assets | 51,804 | 52,478 |
| Net Current Assets | 54,249 | 51,167 |

### Analysis

Total assets increased from ₹1,48,903 crore in FY2025 to ₹1,55,967 crore in FY2026.

Cash and cash equivalents decreased from ₹24,455 crore to ₹22,201 crore.

Total equity decreased from ₹96,203 crore to ₹93,297 crore.

The reduction in equity was influenced by shareholder distributions and the company's ₹18,000 crore share buyback completed during FY2026.

Despite the reduction in cash and equity, Infosys continued to maintain a substantial liquidity position.

## 4. Cash Flow Analysis

| Cash Flow Category | FY 2025 | FY 2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Operating Activities | 35,694 | 33,986 |
| Investing Activities | -1,946 | 1,946 |
| Financing Activities | -24,161 | -39,786 |
| Ending Cash and Cash Equivalents | 24,455 | 22,201 |

### Analysis

Net cash generated from operating activities was ₹33,986 crore in FY2026.

Operating cash flow remained strong, although it decreased from ₹35,694 crore in FY2025.

Investing activities generated net cash of ₹1,946 crore in FY2026 compared with a net cash outflow of ₹1,946 crore in FY2025.

Financing activities used ₹39,786 crore of cash in FY2026. Major financing cash outflows included dividends and the company's share buyback.

The strong operating cash flow indicates that the company continued to generate significant cash from its core business operations.

## 6. Overall Financial Assessment

Based on the FY2026 consolidated financial statements:

1. Revenue increased by 9.6%.
2. Operating profit increased by 5.3%.
3. Net profit increased by 10.2%.
4. EPS increased by 11.0%.
5. ROE was 31.6%.
6. Operating cash flow remained strong at ₹33,986 crore.
7. Total assets increased to ₹1,55,967 crore.
8. Financing cash outflow increased because of dividends and the share buyback.
9. Dividend per share increased to ₹48.

### Conclusion

Infosys showed positive financial performance in FY2026, with growth in revenue, operating profit, net profit and EPS. The company also generated substantial cash from operating activities.

The increase in financing outflows was largely associated with shareholder distributions and the share buyback. Therefore, when evaluating the company, an analyst should consider both its strong operating performance and its capital-allocation decisions.
